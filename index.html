Help Center & Student Support

Frequently asked questions and direct support contact

How do I submit my assessment document? ▾
Open the Tests card, locate the active assessment, and click Upload Solution. You can drag and drop a PDF or PowerPoint presentation (.ppt, .pptx).
How does the faculty keyword-highlighting work? ▾
During grading, faculty can see essential target keywords pre-highlighted in your uploaded document or search custom terms on-the-fly to expedite evaluation.
What should I do if my document upload fails? ▾
Ensure your file is below 25MB and is a standard .pdf, .ppt, or .pptx file. You can also contact support using the form below.
